What is Meal Bridge?

Meal Bridge fights hunger and reduces wastage by linking people and organizations with surplus vegetarian meals to those who need them most.

  • Verified, fresh, vegetarian meals only
  • Easy to donate and request
  • Support for communities and wellbeing
